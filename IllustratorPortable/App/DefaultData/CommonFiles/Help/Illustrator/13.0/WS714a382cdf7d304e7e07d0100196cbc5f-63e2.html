Type / Spelling and language dictionaries

Edit the spelling dictionary

  1. Choose Edit > Edit Custom Dictionary.
  2. Do any of the following, and click Done:

    • To add a word to the dictionary, type the word in the Entry text box, and click Add.

    • To remove a word from the dictionary, select the word in the list, and click Delete.

    • To modify a word in the dictionary, select the word in the list. Then type the new word in the Entry text box, and click Change.
