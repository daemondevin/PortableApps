Superior design features

Live Color

Explore color harmonies and dynamically apply color at once to multiple vector graphics. With Live Color, you can discover new color combinations, quickly test them, and then save and reuse them. You can preview changes to your artwork, shift an artwork’s entire tone by playing with the color wheel, or merely adjust one color with maximum precision. (See Live Color overview.)

Swatches panel

Save a color group to the Swatches panel so you can quickly refer back to a favorite set of colors. (See Save Color Guide colors to the Swatches panel.)

Isolation mode

Use isolation mode to protect areas of an artwork from being edited. You can group, hide, lock, and restack layers with confidence, knowing that isolated portions of your artwork will not be inadvertently changed. (See Isolate groups and sublayers.)
